# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and open to
technical disagreement. Focus feedback on the work and its evidence, not on
people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the project maintainer. Reports will be reviewed
promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove
comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to
protect a welcoming project.
